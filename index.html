Profile

Engineering software across the full lifecycle

My work is centered on software engineering — programming and coding, software architecture, and backend and frontend development — supported by capabilities in product design, UI/UX, documentation, planning, and proposal preparation.

I have designed and implemented enterprise and industrial systems, including a mechanized calculation and labeling system for energy-efficiency certification in the home-appliance sector, developed within Behsaman Energy Group; social and community platforms; and a substantial body of developer infrastructure — frameworks, component libraries, and utilities — built to be reused across real products.

A consistent thread in my practice is automation: browser-automation systems, large-scale web crawlers, and task-specific bots, more recently combined with AI integration and the practical use of agentic AI workflows. I also have hands-on experience managing online businesses — retail storefronts, sales channels, and marketplace operations — which keeps my engineering decisions grounded in commercial reality.

Beyond primary work, I maintain an active interest in game development (Unreal Engine and Unity 3D), regular DevOps practice, and current software and computing trends, from cloud infrastructure and AI to cloud-based gaming and streaming.

Domains of Activity

Where my work has been applied

Enterprise & IndustrialEnergy-sector software systems

At Behsaman Energy Group I worked on the mechanization of technical and regulatory processes: a system that automates the calculations required for issuing national energy-efficiency labels for storage water heaters, covering the calculation engine, field-service tooling connected to measuring equipment, and formal report generation. I also contributed to early-stage IIOT planning — researching and drafting a preliminary design for automated material-rate measurement on gas transmission lines — and prepared the system design and proposal for a warehouse information management platform.

Social & Community PlatformsNetworks where people organize

I have designed and built platforms that coordinate people: Mehrus, a social-cooperation platform delivered for a Tehran-based company, for organizing shared economic needs — joint purchasing and selling, cooperative housing, shared rentals, service marketplaces, and charitable causes — and Bregstar, a content-organization-centered social network specified from proposal through implementation documentation and backend architecture. Related work includes social-network integrations and audience-management automation.

Developer InfrastructureFrameworks, libraries, and tooling

A significant portion of my independent work is infrastructure: Offcode, a batteries-included TypeScript backend framework that has served as the foundation of several real products; Coastomizer, a modular UI component library distributed as roughly twenty npm packages; and Otomat.js, a browser-automation library. These exist because repeated product work exposed the same foundations being rebuilt — so I built them once, properly, and reused them.

E-commerce & Online BusinessRetail operations and marketplace automation

I have managed successful online businesses end to end: two video-game retail stores (Tinoos and WowSource), including sourcing and selling game titles internationally with cryptocurrency payment tooling, operating game servers, and running sales channels and marketplace operations. On the engineering side, I designed and developed e-commerce storefronts for business clients and a product extraction system that harvested over 100,000 items from the Newegg marketplace into cloud storage for downstream sale channels.

Education & AssessmentExamination and evaluation platforms

I developed Testak, an examination platform contracted for a Konkour (Iranian national university entrance exam) institution, and personality-assessment platforms covering instruments such as MBTI — handling both the software engineering and the product and UI/UX design.

Games & Interactive MediaGame development and gaming services

I maintain working knowledge of game development in Unreal Engine and Unity 3D, including narrative design, alongside practical operations experience with game servers and a community gaming platform with its own forum and authentication services. My long-standing involvement in the gaming space — from retail to server operations — gives me an operator's understanding of the domain, not just a developer's.
